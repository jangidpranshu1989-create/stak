Our Story

A Taste of Nigeria, Crafted with Elegance

Founded in 2018, Delight Restaurant brings the vibrant flavours of Nigerian cuisine to Wolverhampton with a refined, contemporary touch.

Our chefs honour traditional recipes — slow-cooked stews, fire-grilled meats, aromatic rice dishes — while presenting them with modern plating and the finest ingredients. Every dish tells a story of heritage, community, and the joy of sharing a meal.
